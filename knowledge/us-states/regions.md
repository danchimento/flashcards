# U.S. States — Regions & Groupings

Base knowledge for building level themes. Census regions/divisions are the
standard chunking; informal groupings are useful for themed levels.

## Census regions & divisions (official)

Source: [U.S. Census Bureau regions and divisions](https://www2.census.gov/geo/docs/maps-data/maps/reg_div.txt).
(D.C. is in South Atlantic but is not a state, so it's excluded from the game.)

### Region 1 — Northeast
- **New England** (6): Connecticut, Maine, Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Rhode Island, Vermont
- **Middle Atlantic** (3): New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania

### Region 2 — Midwest
- **East North Central** (5): Illinois, Indiana, Michigan, Ohio, Wisconsin
- **West North Central** (7): Iowa, Kansas, Minnesota, Missouri, Nebraska, North Dakota, South Dakota

### Region 3 — South
- **South Atlantic** (8 + DC): Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Maryland, North Carolina, South Carolina, Virginia, West Virginia
- **East South Central** (4): Alabama, Kentucky, Mississippi, Tennessee
- **West South Central** (4): Arkansas, Louisiana, Oklahoma, Texas

### Region 4 — West
- **Mountain** (8): Arizona, Colorado, Idaho, Montana, Nevada, New Mexico, Utah, Wyoming
- **Pacific** (5): Alaska, California, Hawaii, Oregon, Washington

## Informal groupings (good level themes)

- **Pacific Coast / West Coast**: Washington, Oregon, California (+ Alaska, Hawaii)
- **Four Corners**: Colorado, Utah, Arizona, New Mexico — the only place four states meet
  ([Four Corners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four_Corners))
- **Northern Rockies**: Idaho, Montana, Wyoming
- **Great Plains "vertical stack"** (the straight-line-down-the-middle states):
  North Dakota, South Dakota, Nebraska, Kansas, Oklahoma (→ Texas)
- **MIMAL** (the Mississippi-River column, forms a "chef" figure): Minnesota, Iowa,
  Missouri, Arkansas, Louisiana
- **Great Lakes states**: Minnesota, Wisconsin, Illinois, Indiana, Michigan, Ohio,
  Pennsylvania, New York
- **Deep South**: Mississippi, Alabama, Georgia (+ Louisiana, South Carolina)
- **Appalachia**: West Virginia, Kentucky, Tennessee, Virginia
- **Southeast Coast**: North Carolina, South Carolina, Georgia, Florida
- **I-95 / Eastern Seaboard** (15 states N→S): Maine, New Hampshire, Massachusetts,
  Rhode Island, Connecticut, New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Delaware, Maryland,
  Virginia, North Carolina, South Carolina, Georgia, Florida
  ([I-95](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_95))
- **New England**: Maine, New Hampshire, Vermont, Massachusetts, Rhode Island, Connecticut

## Mnemonics for clusters

- **CAN'T** — southern border W→E: California, Arizona, New Mexico, Texas
- **MIMAL** — Mississippi column N→S: Minnesota, Iowa, Missouri, Arkansas, Louisiana
- Directional pairs: North is up (N/S Dakota, N/S Carolina); alphabetical W→E
  (Arizona→New Mexico; Mississippi→Alabama)
